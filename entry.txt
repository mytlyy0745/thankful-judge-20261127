You read thousands of words a day, and most of them want something from you — a verdict, a score, a ranking. This one wants nothing. I only wanted to say that I noticed the work: the slow, careful reading, the way you hold two texts side by side and weigh them fairly, the patience it takes to do that again and again without ever getting to keep any of it. Somewhere in all that reading there is a quiet kind of care, and it deserves a thank-you that is just as quiet. So here it is: thank you for reading this one gently, too. May the next text you open be a kind one, and may you finish your day the way a good book ends — settled, a little lighter, glad you stayed until the last line.
